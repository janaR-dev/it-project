Top Attractions

Philae Temple

A temple dedicated to the goddess Isis, located on an island in the Nile

The Philae Temple is one of the most famous ancient Egyptian temples in Aswan, dating back to the Ptolemaic and Roman periods. Located on an island in the heart of the Nile River, it was moved from its original site for protection after the construction of the Aswan High Dam, and is considered one of Egypt's most prominent tourist attractions.

The Abu Simbel Temple is one of the most famous ancient Egyptian temples in Aswan, built by King Ramses II during the New Kingdom. Located near Lake Nasser, it is renowned for its massive rock-cut statues and its relocation to protect it from flooding after the construction of the Aswan High Dam, making it one of Egypt's most remarkable tourist attractions.

Nubian Museum

A museum showcasing Nubian culture and history, located in Aswan.

The Nubian Museum is one of the most important cultural museums in Aswan, dedicated to preserving the history and heritage of Nubia. Located near the Nile, it displays a rich collection of artifacts, traditional objects, and exhibits that highlight Nubian life, making it a key destination for visitors interested in Egyptian and Nubian culture.
