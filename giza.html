sphinx

The Great Sphinx of Giza is a timeless symbol of ancient Egyptian power, standing majestically with the body of a lion and the head of a pharaoh

The Great Sphinx of Giza is one of the oldest and most mysterious monuments in the world, believed to have been built during the reign of Pharaoh Khafre around 2500 BC. Carved from a single block of limestone, it stretches about 73 meters in length and 20 meters in height. The statue represents strength and wisdom, combining the body of a lion with the head of a king. Over thousands of years, it has endured erosion, missing its nose and beard, yet it still stands as a powerful symbol of ancient Egyptian civilization.
